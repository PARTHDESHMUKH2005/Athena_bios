Activity

Watch the steps, not a spinner

While a question runs, Athena lists what it is doing: which sections it found, which model it chose, how much it reserved, and whether the receipt checked out. When it is done, the same list stays with the answer.

Why it matters

Trust you can inspect

Each step records what was decided and why, without storing your document text in the log. If something fails, the list ends where it stopped and says what is safe.

FAQ

Questions people ask.

Why not just use ChatGPT or Claude with a PDF?

Those do not show the cost of each answer, do not check a budget before the call, and give no signed receipt. Athena is for people who want cost control and proof.

How do you know the savings are real?

Cost comes only from signed Prune receipts. We compare Athena with two baselines on the same questions, test the accuracy difference statistically, and publish the results once the live benchmark has run.

What if the AI service is down?

Athena stops safely. It never makes up an answer or bills you twice, and your documents stay saved. Try the failure drill in the workspace above.

What about hidden instructions inside a PDF?

The document is fenced off as untrusted text, Shield is on in Prune, and answers are scanned before they are shown. We test with fifteen injection styles.

Does a summary lose information?

It can. That is why follow-ups also pull in the specific earlier messages that matter.

Does it read scanned PDFs?

Not yet. Scanned pages are flagged. Reading them is planned.

Is my data private?

Your workspace is separate from everyone else's and protected by a private token. You can delete any document or chat at any time.
